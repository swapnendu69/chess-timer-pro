Privacy Policy for Chess Timer Pro

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Chess Timer Pro is designed as an offline digital chess clock application for players, tournaments, and clubs. Your privacy is paramount to us.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Chess Timer Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII). All timer controls, move history, and audio/haptic preferences are handled entirely locally on your device.

2. Permissions

Chess Timer Pro does not request any sensitive permissions on your device:

  • Vibration: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when you press a clock button.
  • Audio: Used exclusively to play local mechanical click tones and tournament time-out buzzers.
  • Wake Lock: Used to prevent your screen from dimming or locking during an active chess match.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

This application does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking tools, analytics, or external telemetry libraries.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is fully safe for players of all ages.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy or the app, please contact the developer via the official repository or support email.
